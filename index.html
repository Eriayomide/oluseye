Featured Project

ALIP
African Legal
Intelligence Platform

ALIP is a live legal research platform I built for African jurisdictions, starting with Nigeria. Ten FastAPI microservices index case law and statutes, and the platform tracks which authorities are still good law through a citator with overruling detection. Case summaries are written by AI but grounded only in the retrieved source text, so it can't invent a citation that doesn't exist. It's live in beta with over 2,100 cases indexed so far.

Featured Project

SchoLink
WhatsApp Front Desk
for African Schools

SchoLink turns a school's WhatsApp number into a full front desk. Parents message in directly and an AI assistant answers questions and looks up fee balances on the spot, while admins handle broadcasts and payment records from a dashboard. Every school is walled off from every other one at the database level, and the AI itself is built so it can never reach into a parent's personal fee data outside of their own conversation.
